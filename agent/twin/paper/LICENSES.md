# Licences and credits

Every shape in this kit was drawn for this kit (hand-authored SVG geometry written by
`src/gen.py`), except the Argos outline, which was vectorised from a CC0 photo (below).
Nothing was extracted, traced or copied from the HackUTA opening-ceremony deck (Canva):
the deck was only looked at for mood (palette family, Greek subjects).

Kit licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), same as its sources.
